# Privacy policy

Karadelik Player ("the app") · last updated 21 October 2026

**The app collects no personal data. We run no servers that receive your data,
and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.**

## What stays on your device

- The sources you add (server address, username, playlist or portal address),
  their channel, film and series lists, the TV guide, favourites, watch history
  and settings are stored in a database on your device only.
- Passwords, MAC addresses and API keys are kept in the operating system's
  credential store (Windows Credential Manager, the iOS Keychain) or, on
  Android, in a file in the app's private storage encrypted with a key held by
  the Android Keystore. They are never written to the database or to logs.
- Encrypted backups are created only when you ask for one and are saved where
  you choose.

## Who the app talks to

The app connects only to services you configure:

- **Your provider** (Xtream Codes server, M3U playlist, Stalker portal, XMLTV
  guide): to load lists and to play streams, sending the credentials you
  entered for that provider.
- **TMDB**, only if you enter your own TMDB API key: the title and year of a
  film or series, to fetch its details and artwork.
- **OpenSubtitles**, only if you enter your own API key: the title you search
  for, to find subtitles.
- **GitHub** (Windows only): to check for and download app updates.

These services handle requests under their own privacy policies. The app does
not send them anything else.

## Children

The app is not directed at children. It provides no content; what is shown
comes from the sources the user adds.

## Deleting your data

Removing a source in Settings deletes its lists, history and stored password.
Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/enesbsafak/kiptvplayer/issues
(for security reports see
https://github.com/enesbsafak/kiptvplayer/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

# Gizlilik politikası (Türkçe)

**Uygulama hiçbir kişisel veri toplamaz. Verilerinizi alan bir sunucumuz yoktur;
uygulamada analiz, reklam veya izleme kodu bulunmaz.**

- Eklediğiniz kaynaklar, listeleri, yayın akışı, favoriler, izleme geçmişi ve
  ayarlar yalnızca cihazınızdaki veritabanında tutulur.
- Şifreler, MAC adresleri ve API anahtarları işletim sisteminin anahtar
  deposunda (Windows Kimlik Bilgisi Yöneticisi, iOS Anahtar Zinciri) ya da
  Android'de, anahtarı Android Keystore'da duran şifreli bir dosyada saklanır;
  veritabanına veya kayıtlara yazılmaz.
- Uygulama yalnızca sizin eklediğiniz sağlayıcıya, kendi anahtarınızı
  girerseniz TMDB ve OpenSubtitles'a ve (yalnızca Windows'ta) güncellemeler
  için GitHub'a bağlanır.
- Bir kaynağı silmek listelerini, geçmişini ve şifresini siler; uygulamayı
  kaldırmak tüm verilerini cihazdan siler.
- İletişim: https://github.com/enesbsafak/kiptvplayer/issues
